Use cases by domain

Open your domain to see the recurring pain, where MorphoGrid fits, and the exact sequence that turns the incoming file into something useful.

$Finance & accountingMonthly packs, reconciliations, budget extracts and ledger exports+

The painpoint

Reports arrive with title rows, inconsistent cost centres, date formats and duplicate transaction references. Rebuilding the cleanup every month creates rework and weak auditability.

The use case

Standardize recurring finance exports before reconciliation, variance analysis, pivoting or handoff to the reporting workbook.

Steps in MorphoGrid

  1. Load the XLSX or delimited export.
  2. Remove introductory rows and promote the real header.
  3. Normalize dates, account labels and numeric types.
  4. Validate required IDs and deduplicate references.
  5. Create a region, entity or cost-centre pivot.
  6. Save the recipe and export the clean workbook.
◎Sales & revenue operationsCRM exports, pipeline hygiene, territories and account lists+

The painpoint

Customer names, stages, owner fields and territories drift over time. Duplicate accounts and inconsistent email or phone formats make imports unreliable and dashboards misleading.

The use case

Prepare CRM extracts for territory planning, campaign handoff, pipeline reviews or clean re-import without spreadsheet formula chains.

Steps in MorphoGrid

  1. Open the CRM CSV or Excel export.
  2. Trim and standardize names, emails and statuses.
  3. Build nested filters for territory and stage rules.
  4. Fill required defaults and validate owner fields.
  5. Remove duplicate account or contact IDs.
  6. Save a reusable CRM hygiene recipe.
◌Marketing & growthCampaign reports, lead files, UTM cleanup and channel analysis+

The painpoint

Every platform exports different campaign names, dates and performance columns. Manual copying hides channel comparisons and creates yet another version of the report.

The use case

Normalize multi-channel exports for campaign reporting, lead routing, attribution review and a consistent weekly performance view.

Steps in MorphoGrid

  1. Load a platform export and remove summary rows.
  2. Use regex and text tools to normalize campaign names.
  3. Calculate shared metrics such as CPL or conversion rate.
  4. Append compatible channel files when needed.
  5. Infer a campaign, channel or region chart.
  6. Export and reuse the recipe next reporting cycle.
◇People operations & HRRosters, survey responses, training records and benefits files+

The painpoint

Employee files contain missing departments, mismatched identifiers, inconsistent dates and sensitive columns that should not be included in every downstream handoff.

The use case

Prepare operational HR files for surveys, training compliance, headcount summaries or vendor upload while keeping transformation work local.

Steps in MorphoGrid

  1. Load the roster or response file locally.
  2. Select only the columns needed for the purpose.
  3. Normalize names, departments and employment dates.
  4. Mask or remove sensitive fields before sharing.
  5. Validate employee IDs and required values.
  6. Create a department summary and export.
▦Operations & procurementVendor feeds, inventory files, purchase orders and fulfilment data+

The painpoint

Suppliers use different delimiters, SKU conventions, units and column names. Teams lose time translating formats before comparing prices, stock or delivery performance.

The use case

Convert irregular supplier files into one validated structure for purchasing, inventory review, exception handling or system upload.

Steps in MorphoGrid

  1. Choose the vendor file and specify its delimiter.
  2. Rename and reorder fields into your standard schema.
  3. Clean SKUs with text or regex transformations.
  4. Convert quantity, price and date fields.
  5. Look up reference values or append another feed.
  6. Save a vendor-specific recipe and export.
♡Customer support & successTicket exports, feedback themes, SLA reviews and account health+

The painpoint

Ticket exports mix tags, free text, timestamps and account IDs. The useful signal stays buried while teams manually group the same issues each week.

The use case

Steps in MorphoGrid

  6. Export the summary and save the weekly recipe.
✓Risk, audit & complianceEvidence extracts, exception lists, access reviews and control testing+

The painpoint

Evidence files arrive from multiple systems with inconsistent schemas. Manual edits are hard to reproduce, and silent row deletion can undermine confidence in the result.

The use case

Create inspectable preparation workflows for exception testing, access certification, audit sampling and evidence handoff.

Steps in MorphoGrid

  1. Import the source extract without altering the original.
  2. Apply explicit selection, type and validation steps.
  3. Use grouped filters to isolate exceptions.
  4. Review step diagnostics and removed-row samples.
  5. Export the transformed dataset and Audit ZIP.
  6. Save the recipe as the repeatable control procedure.
